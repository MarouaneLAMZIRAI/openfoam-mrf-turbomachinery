## Prediction assumptions

The affinity-law predictor implements same-geometry speed scaling: flow is
proportional to rotational speed, pressure/head and torque to speed squared,
and power to speed cubed. These relationships require a calibrated reference
point and similar operating regime. They do not replace CFD or experiment and
do not automatically account for efficiency, cavitation, or Reynolds-number
changes.
